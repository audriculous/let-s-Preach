            Passage fetcher

            Bible Gateway does not let web apps read its pages directly, so the app goes through a small relay. It tries free public relays first. For reliable fetching, set up your own free Cloudflare Worker (see the README) and paste its URL here.

            Preaching roster

            Paste the link to your roster in Google Sheets. Easiest: in Sheets choose File, Share, Publish to web, pick the roster tab and "Comma-separated values (.csv)", then copy that link. It updates itself whenever the sheet changes. The sheet needs headings like Date, Preacher and Passage (Series and Title are used if there).

            Roster in Excel? from Files instead. Choose it again when the roster changes.

            Only have it as a Google Doc, an email or on paper? . You check the rows before anything is saved.

            Weekly prep plan

            Your usual week. Each sermon starts with it once it has a date, and you can change any sermon's plan on its own (Plan tab in the study).

            Sync and backup

            Uses the same Worker to keep your iPad and laptop in step, and saves a backup of every study once a day (the last 30 days are kept). Put the same sync code on each device. Keep it private: anyone with it can see your studies.
